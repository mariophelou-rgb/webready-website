Questions people ask

What do I need to send you?

Your company profile is enough. If you don't have one, send your business name, what you do, your services, contact details, logo and a few photos.

How long does it take?

10 to 15 days from the day we receive your profile. Most of that time is you reviewing and us making your changes.

Can I change things after I see it?

Yes. You see the real website before it goes live and tell us what you don't like. We change it.

Will my website show up on Google?

We set up the basics Google needs: page titles, descriptions and your business details. Ranking high takes time, and a Google Business Profile helps a lot. We'll show you how to set one up.

Is the website mine?

Yes. It runs on your own domain name, registered in your name.

Can my website be in Arabic, English or French?

Yes, in one language or all three. Tell us which ones you need when you ask for your price.
